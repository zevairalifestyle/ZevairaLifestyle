Customer Trust & Security

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 28 September 2026

1. Introduction

At ZevairaLifestyle, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy details the straightforward ways we collect, utilize, and protect your information when visiting our website or ordering our handcrafted fragrances.

2. Information We Collect

When placing an order or contacting our concierge, we collect the necessary details required to deliver your fragrance parcel safely:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, mobile telephone number (for WhatsApp dispatch notifications), and optional email address.
  • Delivery Address: Complete street address, city, and postal code for courier handoff.
  • Order Details: Selected perfumes, bottle sizes, quantities, and optional custom gift card notes.
  • Payment Information: For online transfers (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, UPaisa, Bank Transfer), transaction screenshots or reference numbers for verification. We never store credit card numbers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for practical e-commerce fulfillment purposes:

  • Fulfilling, packaging, and dispatching your perfume orders.
  • Generating courier booking slips via our shipping partners (TCS, Call Courier, Trax).
  • Sending pre-dispatch verification messages and tracking IDs via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Assisting with product queries, scent exchanges, and customer support.

4. No Sharing or Selling of Data

We hold your trust sacred. ZevairaLifestyle will never sell, lease, or trade your personal telephone numbers, addresses, or purchase history to third-party telemarketers or external advertising networks. Your contact details are shared exclusively with our courier delivery partners for doorstep parcel arrival.

5. Browser Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses standard client-side browser storage (HTML5 localStorage) to ensure a seamless shopping experience. This safely retains your active shopping cart items, saved wishlist perfumes, and dark/light visual theme preference on your device without transmitting tracking beacons.

6. WhatsApp Communications

When you click "Send Order on WhatsApp" or message our support number (+92 300 1234567), standard WhatsApp end-to-end encryption applies. We only communicate with you regarding your active inquiries, order verification, or requested scent guidance.
