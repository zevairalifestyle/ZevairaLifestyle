The Engineering of Scents

Why Zevaira Outlasts the Heat

Most imported perfumes are formulated in cool, dry European climates. When worn under 42°C heat in Lahore or the 85% relative humidity of Karachi, their top notes evaporate within 30 minutes, leaving a faint skin scent.

Zevaira perfumes are engineered with heavy molecular fixatives, authentic aged ambergris, and dense wood resins that anchor to the skin and cotton kurtas. We formulate strictly at Extrait de Parfum concentration (30% to 38% pure perfume oils).

Pure imported fragrance compounds with 6 weeks maturation.
