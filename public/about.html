The Climate Challenge

Why Traditional Perfumes Disappear

For decades, perfume enthusiasts in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad have paid exorbitant prices for designer bottles created in Paris or Milan.

Those formulas were engineered for European temperatures averaging 15°C to 22°C. When exposed to the scorching dry winds of Multan or the 85% humidity of Clifton, light alcohol carriers vaporize rapidly. The volatile top notes vanish into the air before you even step into your car.

At Zevaira, we rewrote the olfactory rulebook for our climate.

The Zevaira Formulation

We eliminate excessive alcohol dilution. By blending at Extrait de Parfum concentration (30% to 38% pure perfume oils), we anchor the scent directly onto your skin and fabrics.
