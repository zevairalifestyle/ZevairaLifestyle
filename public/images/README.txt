ZevairaLifestyle - Product Images Directory
===========================================

To use your real perfume photography instead of the inline SVG illustrations:
1. Save your photos here (for example: royal-vetiver.jpg, jasmine-blanc.jpg, etc.)
2. Open js/products.js
3. In any product entry, update the "image" field:
   image: "images/royal-vetiver.jpg"
4. Save the file. The website will automatically display your real photo with smooth rounded corners and luxury drop shadows!
